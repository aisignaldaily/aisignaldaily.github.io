FIRST, WRITE ONE BRAND FILEEvery AI helper reads the same file, so everything sounds like you. Copy this into a Google Doc, fill it in once, and paste it at the start of every AI chat (or into a ChatGPT Project / Claude Project so it's always there).

BUSINESS: [name], [what you sell], [city / area served]
CUSTOMERS: [who buys, their top 3 problems, words they use]
OFFER: [main services + prices or price ranges]
VOICE: [3 words, e.g. friendly, direct, local]. Never say: [words you hate]
PROOF: [real reviews, years in business, numbers you can stand behind]
BOOKING: [your booking link] · HOURS: [hours] · PHONE: [number]
RULES: no fake reviews, no made-up results, no prices I didn't list.

DON'T WANT 10 TOOLS? START WITH 3

  1. Writer + Scheduler. One hour on Sunday: ChatGPT or Claude writes the week, Buffer posts it.
  2. Booker. A booking link in your bio and every post, so interest turns into appointments.
  3. DM helper. One keyword ("PRICE", "BOOK") that sends your link automatically.

Rough setup time for these three: an afternoon (our estimate). Add one more helper each week.

WHAT STILL NEEDS YOUAI moves the information. You run the business.

  • Approving what goes out. Read posts and replies before they go live, at least at the start.
  • Prices, discounts and promises. Never let AI quote prices or deadlines you didn't set.
  • Real conversations. Hot leads, complaints and anything personal go to a human.
  • Honesty. No fake reviews or made-up results; it breaks platform rules and trust.
  • Privacy and consent. Only message people who contacted you or opted in.

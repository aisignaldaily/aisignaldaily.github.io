🚀NEW TO AI? START HEREYou don't need all 50 tools. Pick the job that eats most of your time, find its section, and try one tool this week. If you're brand new, start with these five:

  1. One chat assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini). It drafts emails, summarizes documents and answers questions. Pick one and use it every day for a week.
  2. Perplexity for research. It answers with links to its sources, so you can check them.
  3. NotebookLM for your own documents. Upload PDFs or notes and ask questions about them.
  4. Canva for social posts, flyers and presentations, with AI built in.
  5. Fathom for meetings. It records, transcribes and summarizes your calls so you stop taking notes.

A simple first week

  • Write down the one task you'd most like to never do again, and find its tool above

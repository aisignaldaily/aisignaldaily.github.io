# AI Signal Daily - Viral Style Guide v2 (Nov 2026)

Read this before building any post. Where it differs from the run instructions, THIS FILE WINS on style; the run instructions win on rules (no rumors, no real-people likenesses, AI visuals: men allowed (fictional, fully and professionally dressed), never women or female figures, never nudity, vulgar or suggestive content, no gambling/alcohol, sources, labels).

## What Instagram rewards right now
1. Watch time / completion (Reels) - hook must land in the first 1.5 s.
2. SENDS (DM shares) - the strongest signal for reaching NON-followers (weighted far above likes).
3. Saves, then likes per reach.
4. Originality - our own design and commentary on top of sources, never plain reposts.
5. Text on slides, captions and alt text are read for search (Instagram SEO) - use plain keywords.

## Pick stories by "would someone send this to a friend?"
Rank story ideas by shareability, not importance. Best performers on top AI pages this month:
- **AI said/did something wild** ("Grok called humans 'bags of meat'") - personality + surprise.
- **Someone asked AI X** - relatable experiment with a surprising answer.
- **Free resource worth $$$** ("free 28-min masterclass beats $300 courses") - save + send bait.
- **The internet reacts** - news + 3-5 slides of the funniest/best reactions (summarize real public reactions in our own words; never invent reactions, never show real people's photos or names of private individuals).
- **"This changes X for everyone"** - big launch explained in 1 sentence, why it matters to normal people.
- **Hot takes** - a bold, defensible opinion beats a neutral summary ("Hot take" hooks average far more views than "story" hooks).
- Memes / relatable AI humor - about 1 in 4 posts.
Avoid: dry funding news, benchmarks without a "so what", anything that needs more than one sentence to understand.

## Carousel cover (slide 1) - the "Evolving" formula, adapted
- Top 55-60%: one strong visual. Options in order: (a) official product screenshot / UI / press image of a PRODUCT (no people), (b) bold graphic or chart, (c) Higgsfield AI image (robots, devices, abstract tech, or a fictional man fully dressed in a work setting; never women, never real people; labeled "Made with AI").
- Bottom 40-45%: GIANT all-caps headline, white Anton, tight leading, 2-4 key words in mint #3DF5C8, max ~14 words, fills the width. Thin dark gradient behind text.
- Tiny "SWIPE ⬅️" tag bottom-right; small "@aisignaldaily.news" + mint signal mark bottom-left.
- The headline alone must make someone stop and want to send it.

## Slides 2-6
- One idea per slide, 6-25 words, big text. Alternate layouts every slide (stat card, quote card, before/after, mini chart, "what it means for you").
- Slide 2 must pay off the hook immediately (no slow intro).
- Second-to-last: "Why it matters / what you can do today".
- Last: "Send this to someone who [specific person: 'still writes emails by hand', 'thinks AI is a fad']" + "Comment AI for today's tools" + follow.

## Reels (evening)
- 12-20 s. Text on screen from frame 1 (most watch muted). First 1.5 s = the hook as giant text; no logo intro.
- A visual change every 2-3 s (cut, zoom, new text card, clip). Big captions centered in the safe area.
- End on a loop-friendly frame (last frame flows into the first) to boost replays.
- Higgsfield clips (when connected): 3-5 s b-roll beats behind text - robots, devices, futuristic workspaces, abstract data visuals. May include a fictional man (fully, professionally dressed, e.g. at a desk, in a lab, using a device). Never women or female figures, never real people or lookalikes, never nudity, vulgar or suggestive content. Max ONE Higgsfield clip per day (~35 credits, seedance_2_5, 9:16, 5 s), only when there is no official footage; check balance first and skip if under 100 credits. Label "Made with AI" in caption and on screen corner.
- Official company demo clips (computer runs) beat everything for launches - always credit.

## Captions
- Line 1: hook rephrased with a keyword (e.g. "OpenAI's new agent just did X").
- 3-5 short lines: what happened, why it matters, one surprising detail. Use plain search words (AI tool names, "ChatGPT", "AI agent", "free AI tools").
- "Source: ..."
- SEND trigger: "Send this to a friend who ___" (vary it).
- "Comment AI and we'll DM you today's tools + our free AI toolkit 👇"
- A question that is easy to answer in 1-3 words ("Would you trust it? Yes or no").
- 3-6 relevant hashtags max (#AI #ArtificialIntelligence + 2-4 topic tags).
- Alt text on every image: a plain description with keywords.

## Hook formulas (rotate; never reuse the same opener twice in a day)
- "[AI/company] just [did unexpected thing]."
- "Someone asked [AI] to ___. The answer is wild."
- "This free [tool/course] replaces a $___ [thing]."
- "[Big company] quietly changed ___ for everyone."
- "Hot take: ___."
- "AI can now ___ in [seconds]. Here's how."
- "Stop doing ___. AI does it in 10 seconds."
- "The internet is losing it over ___."

## Volume
Three posts a day now. Quality > volume, but if usage allows later, add a 4th light post (meme/humor) at midday.


## v3 additions (Nov 25, 2026)
- Visual continuity: every carousel uses ONE matched image series (same background, palette, lighting, object family) and every Reel uses ONE continuous scene (same subject/set, only camera/action changes). Never a slideshow of unrelated scenes.
- Sound: Reels always get a custom sound bed with NO music: cinematic sound design or dramatic nature ambience matched to the story (thunder/wind for big news, rain/ocean for calm useful tips, deep drone/hum for futuristic). Keep it under the visuals, slight lift on the hook.
- Carousels can't carry sound via API. If a story is very visual and dramatic, it may be made as a short slides-with-motion Reel with sound instead.
- Progress marker ("1/4") on every Reel beat and carousel slide.
- How to make it: see tools/HIGGSFIELD.md.
